RULE 01 · CORE

Countable vs Uncountable

Ең алдымен noun-ның түрін анықта

COUNTABLE

Жеке санауға болады. Singular және plural формалары бар.

a book → two books
an idea → three ideas
a student → many students
UNCOUNTABLE

Әдетте жеке бірлік ретінде тікелей саналмайды. Көпше -s алмайды.

water · money · advice · information · furniture
GrammarCountableUncountable
a/an✓ a book✕ an information
plural -s✓ books✕ informations
many✓ many books✕ many money
much✕ much books✓ much money
TEST 01

We need more ___ before making a decision.

A) informations
B) information
C) an information
D) informationes
Жауабы мен талдауы
B) information. Information — uncountable noun: a/an қолданылмайды және plural -s алмайды.

How many + countable plural: How many students?
How much + uncountable: How much money?
Much / many: әсіресе сұраулы және болымсыз сөйлемдерде жиі кездеседі: Do you have many books? We don't have much time. Affirmative сөйлемде a lot of өте табиғи: We have a lot of work.

RULE 04 · SOME / ANY

Some & Any

Countable plural және uncountable noun-мен

SOME

Көбіне affirmative сөйлемде.

some books · some water · some information
ANY

Көбіне questions және negative сөйлемдерде.

any books · any water · any information
Маңызды: ұсыныс пен өтініште question болса да some қолданыла алады: Would you like some tea? · Can I have some water?

RULE 05 · TOO / ENOUGH

Too much · Too many · Enough

Шамадан көп және жеткілікті

TOO MANY + countable plural
too many students
too many mistakes
TOO MUCH + uncountable
too much money
too much work
ENOUGH + noun: enough books · enough chairs · enough money · enough time.

RULE 04 · MEANING

A few ≠ few · A little ≠ little

«Аз, бірақ жеткілікті» және «тым аз» айырмасы

A FEW + countable

Аз болса да бар / жеткілікті.

I have a few friends here.
FEW

Өте аз, жеткіліксіз.

Few students understood it.
A LITTLE + uncountable

Аз болса да бар / жеткілікті.

We have a little time.
LITTLE

Өте аз, жеткіліксіз.

We have little time.

RULE 06 · MEANING CHANGE

Both countable & uncountable

Мағынасына қарай түрі өзгереді

glassglass = шыны материал; a glass = стақан
paperpaper = қағаз; a paper = мақала/жазбаша жұмыс
hairhair = шаш жалпы; a hair = бір тал шаш
chickenchicken = тауық еті; a chicken = бір тауық
roomroom = бос орын; a room = бөлме
timetime = уақыт; times = рет (three times); a time = кезең/уақыт (There was a time…)
Restaurant English: сусын атауы негізінен uncountable болуы мүмкін, бірақ тапсырыс кезінде two coffees = two cups of coffee деп айтыла алады.

High-frequency traps

Тестте бірден тану керек

ҚатеДұрысНеге?

5-second algorithm:
1) Noun countable ма?
2) Countable болса singular/plural?
3) Singular countable → a/an мүмкін.
4) Plural countable → many / (a) few.
5) Uncountable → much / (a) little.
6) some / any / a lot of → екеуімен де қолданыла алады.
FINAL REVIEW · 15 QUESTIONS

Exam Practice

1. I need some ___ about the programme.

A) informations
B) information
C) an information
D) informationes
Жауабы
B. Information — uncountable.

2. How ___ books did you buy?

A) much
B) many
C) little
D) an
Жауабы
B. Books — countable plural.

3. We don't have ___ time.

A) many
B) much
C) a few
D) several
Жауабы
B. Time — uncountable in this meaning.

4. She has ___ close friends, so she isn't lonely.

A) a few
B) few
C) a little
D) much
Жауабы
A. Friends countable; positive meaning → a few.

5. There is ___ hope of success; the situation is very bad.

A) a few
B) few
C) a little
D) little
Жауабы
D. Hope uncountable; negative “almost none” → little.

6. Which is correct?

A) an advice
B) advices
C) a piece of advice
D) many advice
Жауабы
C. A piece of advice.

7. The ___ is good today.

A) weathers
B) weather
C) a weather
D) many weather
Жауабы
B. Weather — uncountable.

8. The news ___ surprising.

A) are
B) were
C) is
D) have
Жауабы
C. News әдетте singular verb алады.

9. We bought ___ furniture for the office.

A) many
B) a lot of
C) a few
D) furnitures
Жауабы
B. Furniture uncountable; a lot of екеуімен де жүреді.

10. There are ___ students in the library today.

A) much
B) a little
C) many
D) an
Жауабы
C. Students — countable plural.

11. I drank ___ water after the run.

A) a few
B) a little
C) many
D) several
Жауабы
B. Water — uncountable.

12. She bought two ___ of bread.

A) loafs
B) loaves
C) breads
D) breades
Жауабы
B. two loaves of bread.

13. The vase is made of ___.

A) a glass
B) glasses
C) glass
D) many glass
Жауабы
C. Glass = material, uncountable.

14. Can I have ___ of water?

A) a glass
B) a water
C) many glass
D) waters
Жауабы
A. a glass of water.

15. There is ___ hair on my jacket.

A) a
B) an
C) many
D) much
Жауабы
A. Бір тал шаш → a hair.
